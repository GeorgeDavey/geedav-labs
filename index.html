Threads
of Life

A life simulator that runs in a web browser. You live one life from start to end, and the game tells it as a memoir, with one chapter for each year.

Infancy, childhood, teens, young adult, adulthood, middle age, later years.

How a year works

  1. A momentThe year may ask you one question. A year with no question is a quiet year.
  2. Your eveningsYou have a small number of free evenings. You spend each one on a card: time with a person, something at work, or something for yourself.
  3. A resultAfter each evening, one line says how it went and what changed.
  4. A chapterAt the end of the year, the memoir gets a new page with a title and what happened.

Home life and keeping up a hobby happen without using an evening. When the life ends, an epilogue looks back over all of it.

What the game is trying to do

The main aim is for the game's systems to surprise the player with how deep and true to life they are. Depth here means systems that act on each other. For example:

A move to a new club takes your family away from your partner's job.

A new manager comes in and does not rate you.

The memoir is how the game is told, not the main aim. How often things happen is set to make a better story, not to match national figures.

What is in the game

People
Friends and family can slowly grow apart from you if you leave them be. When someone's life changes, a card about it may come up.
Romance and family
A relationship moves through steps, and can lead to children.
Work
Each career follows a track. On the Ladder track you move up through standing and promotion. On the Sport track you play seasons, and your form and your body decide how it goes.
Team sport
Four divisions, clubs that go up and down, contracts, offers from other clubs, and managers who can be sacked.
Individual sport
A world ranking in place of a league.
The town
A town paper that prints only in a year when you make the news or something important happens.

Also: hobbies, school results, money, and a business of your own.

How it is built

  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step and no packages.
  • Runs offline. There is also a one-file version.
  • Old saves keep working when the game changes.
  • Tests, and a script that plays lives by itself to check how they unfold.
  • Built with Claude Code.
